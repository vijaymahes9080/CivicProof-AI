# CivicProof AI - Responsible Use & Governance Notice

## 1. Non-Custodial Boundaries

CivicProof AI is an **informational and pre-verification assistant**. It adheres to the following non-negotiable boundaries:

1. **No Application Submission:** CivicProof AI never submits forms or creates user accounts on government portals. It routes citizens to verified `.gov.in` endpoints.
2. **No Fee Collection:** Indian government scholarships are free to apply for on official portals. CivicProof AI never collects fees or financial account credentials.
3. **No Document Custody:** Identity documents (Aadhaar, income certificates, marksheets) are not uploaded or stored in the database.
4. **Advisory Nature:** Eligibility calculations represent a pre-application check based on published gazettes; the official issuing department retains sole authority for final disbursement.
